## Disclaimers

We reserve the right to update, delete, mangle, and marginalize any portion of
this without notice!

### Liability

The conference and venue accept no responsibility for missing or stolen items.
Storage of personal belongings is offered at attendees' own risk.

### Alcohol on Premises

Beverages will be available through the venue. Intoxicated or disruptive
attendees may be removed. All applicable laws regarding alcohol consumption
are enforced.

### Anti-Harassment Policy

HushCon prohibits harassment based on gender, sexual orientation, disability,
physical appearance, body size, race, or religion. Prohibited conduct includes
offensive comments, intimidation, stalking, unwelcome photography, disruption
of events, and inappropriate contact. Violators may be removed.

### Zombie Outbreaks

In the event of a zombie outbreak during this conference, organizers shall
determine the re-organization and acceptance of attendees based on available
resources, enginuity, natural aptitude in crisis situations, and mental
stability.
